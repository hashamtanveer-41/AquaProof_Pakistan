Punjab & KPK, Pakistan

90% of Pakistan's water feeds the fields. Most of it is wasted.

Farmers irrigate by habit or calendar — not by what their crop actually needs. When canals run late and rains fail, there is no system to decide what to do next.

40–45% irrigation efficiency — over half the water applied to fields is lost to over-irrigation and poor timing.International Water Management Institute

The district office

An officer opens AquaProof and draws the farm.

Using the government dashboard, an agricultural officer traces the exact boundary of a farm on the map. The system records the polygon, calculates field area automatically, and links it to the farmer's crop, soil type, and irrigation sources.

No field visit required for data — weather, crop stage, and evapotranspiration are computed from live APIs and the planting date alone.

The FAO-56 engine

How much water does this crop need today?

The system pulls real weather for the farm's GPS location — temperature, humidity, rainfall, evapotranspiration. The FAO-56 water-balance model runs. The answer is not generic. It is specific to this farm, this crop, this week.

The advisory

The canal is three days away. Should the farmer pump now?

AquaProof weighs the next canal turn, the tubewell pumping cost, and the forecast. It selects the most cost-effective strategy and sends a personalized advisory — by email today, by SMS for the government rollout.

"Aap ke khet ko kal pani ki zaroorat hai." Plain-language recommendations in the farmer's own words.

The farmer replies

The farmer replies. AquaProof understands.

"Mein kal 2 ghantay tube well chalaya tha." The AI reads the message, extracts the irrigation event, updates the farm's water-balance record, and sends back a fresh recommendation. No app download. No smartphone required.

The numbers behind AquaProof

You have seen the journey. Here are the facts, the science, and the team that built it.

The water crisis in numbers

  • 93% of Pakistan's freshwater is consumed by agriculture — more than almost any country on Earth. Pakistan Council of Research in Water Resources
  • 40–45% average irrigation efficiency in Pakistan. Over half the water applied to crops is lost to poor timing and over-irrigation. International Water Management Institute
  • 22 million farming households depend on canal and tubewell irrigation with no farm-specific advisory system. Agricultural Census Pakistan
  • FAO-56 the UN Food & Agriculture Organization's crop-water methodology — the global gold standard for irrigation science — powers our calculations. Allen et al., FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56, 1998

How it works

  1. Register the farm. An officer draws the field boundary on the map. Area is auto-calculated from the GPS polygon saved in the database.
  2. Record the crop. Crop type, planting date, soil profile, and water sources are entered once and stored permanently — full history preserved after harvest.
  3. Pull real weather. Open-Meteo provides temperature, humidity, rainfall, and reference ET₀ for that farm's exact coordinates. No generic district data.
  4. Run the science. FAO-56 water-balance calculates today's irrigation need. The canal vs. tubewell optimizer picks the most cost-effective strategy for this farmer.
  5. Communicate and update. A personalized advisory is sent to the farmer. They reply in Urdu, Roman Urdu, or English. AI reads it, updates the record, recalculates.
